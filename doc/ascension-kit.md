# Ascension Kit

## Tools
- Bag of Holding — inventory management
- Unicorn Horn — cures stun/confusion/blindness/hallucination/sickness
- 7 Candles — Candelabrum of Invocation
- Pick-axe — digging/pathing
- Magic Markers — writing scrolls
- Can of Grease — greased helm/cloak resists grab/strip from mind flayers, eels, krakens
- Blindfold — Medusa alternative to reflection
- Wand of Digging — Earth Plane navigation (nice-to-have)

## Artifacts
- Excalibur — drain resistance

## Armor (+4 or better)
- Silver Dragon Scale Mail (SDSM) — reflection (Shield of Reflection is a valid alt, frees the body slot for a lighter suit like mithril coat)
- Cloak of Magic Resistance — magic resistance
- Gauntlets of Power — +25 St, to-damage, and touch-stoning protection (supersedes plain Gloves — any glove-slot item blocks touch-stoning)
- Rustproof Helm — plain; Helm of Brilliance is a common alt for +Int/+Wis if spell-focused (skippable for non-casters, e.g. Valkyrie)
- Speed boots — mobility
- T-shirt / Hawaiian shirt — extra AC, shirt slot

## Amulets
- Amulet of Life Saving
- Amulet of Flying
- Amulet of Magic Breathing
- Amulet of Reflection — optional/redundant if SDSM is worn

## Rings
- Ring of Conflict — crowd control, trivializes many encounters
- Ring of Free Action — paralysis
- Ring of Levitation — optional; boots of levitation also work (a potion is not a reliable substitute)

## Wands
- Wand of Death — instant-kill offense
- Wand of Polymorph — polypiling
- Wand of Fire — green slime cure/self-zap
- Wand of Striking — drawbridge control

## Potions
- Potion of Full Healing — stockpiled
- Gather unidentified/unwanted potions — dilute with water as needed, feeds the water supply used for blanking scrolls
- Holy Water — drop potion(s) of water on an aligned altar and pray to bless them; required for blessing Bag of Holding and Unicorn Horn specifically; bless as much of the kit as possible, since Lich encounters can curse your gear

## Scrolls
- Scroll of Charging — recharges Magic Markers and Wand of Death; skip on Wand of Wishing (too risky/not worth it)
- Scroll of Genocide — target priority (1 blessed scroll per target in UnNetHack):
  1. Mind Flayers — brain-eating instakill risk
  2. Master Mind Flayers — brain-eating instakill risk
  3. Disenchanters — drains charges/enchantment, wrecks your kit
  4. Vorpal Jabberwocks — ~5% instant decapitation
  5. Weeping Angels — species-level genocide (class 'A' is mostly immune, but this monster was explicitly flagged genocidable)
  6. Weeping Archangels — same as above
  - Note: demons (class '&') are not genocidable at all in this build — none of them carry the genocidable flag, regular or unique.
- Scroll of Remove Curse — worth holding onto
- Gather unidentified/unwanted scrolls — dip in water to blank, then Magic Marker into Enchant Armor / Genocide as needed

## Light/Wish
- Magic Lamp — light source; rubbing can summon a djinni for a last-ditch wish

## Food/Corpses
- Lizard corpse — stoning cure, never rots

## Consumables/Utility
- Self-inflicted confusion source (booze, forgotten spell) — used to repair damaged armor
- Gold from Fort Ludios — funds ~9 points of protection AC

## Luck
- Luckstone — carried until the Orb of Fate is obtained, then dropped (superseded)

## Intrinsics via corpse-eating (non-exhaustive)
- Telepathy — floating eye corpse (kill at range, never in melee, to avoid the paralysis touch)
- Poison resistance — naga corpse
- Sleep resistance — elf corpse
- Fire resistance — fire giant, fire ant, pyrolisk corpse
- Shock resistance — electric eel, flesh golem, gray ooze corpse
- Disintegration resistance — black dragon corpse
- Cold resistance — blue jelly corpse (Valkyries also get this as a role intrinsic)
- Invisibility — invisible stalker corpse
- See invisible — invisible stalker corpse

## Level gain
- Wraith corpse — eating grants an experience level
- Potions of Gain Level
- Succubus/Incubus kills — solid XP in a pinch

## Invocation Requirements (to reach Moloch's Sanctum)
- **Bell of Opening (Silver Bell)** — quest reward; quest requires character level 14
- **Candelabrum of Invocation** — from Vlad's Tower; needs the 7 Candles lit to use
- **Book of the Dead** — dropped by Rodney (Wizard of Yendor) on death
- **Ritual** — performed on the vibrating square at the bottom of Gehennom, using all three items above

## Gehennom Readiness Threshold
- AC of -30 or better before entering — full resistance set required (fire, cold, shock, poison, sleep, disintegration, drain, magic, stoning-avoidance)
- AC stack: +4-or-better armor pieces, protection (~9 pts from Fort Ludios gold), and stacked blessed Scrolls of Enchant Armor (written via Magic Marker) — typically lands around -5, occasionally as low as -42 on a good run
- At that range, most physical attacks simply can't connect — melee threats stop mattering, leaving magic/gaze/breath as the real remaining danger (handled by the resistance set and reflection)
- With this bar met, Vlad's Tower is a formality on the way to grab the Candelabrum, not a real obstacle
